America on the move: metro and rural migration

Where are Americans moving to, and away from? These maps track net domestic migration — people moving between US counties, excluding births, deaths, and international arrivals — across the post-pandemic years 2021 to 2025. The pattern: a steady outflow from the largest metros toward smaller metros, suburbs, and rural counties, concentrated in the Sun Belt.
